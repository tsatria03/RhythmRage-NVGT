Rhythm rage!

Welcome to rhythm rage. This manual is short and simple, as each level in the game has its own playing tutorial, but here are the basics.

This game is based on rhythm and music. The objective in this game is to follow the rhythm of the music as best you can, according to the instructions you are given by the tutorial in each game.
If you are unable to follow the instructions correctly, you will not unlock the next level. At the end of each level, you will get a percentage score and you will be told how well you did.

The only extra keyboard shortcut you need to worry about is the escape key. If you are frustrated, you can quit the game by pressing escape.

You can create your own levels for this game, by looking at the creator's manual. Please take a good look at this manual and use the level creator tool, as this is the best way to find the correct timing for levels.
Use the debug mode to make sure you got the times right before submitting your pack to the public!
To submit a pack, go to oriolgomez.com and find the appropriate place in the games section. You will be able to upload your pack folder as a .zip extension.
To play packs created by other users, first download them from the website and put the .pack file in your packs folder accessible either via the shortcut in your programs folder or, if you are playing a portable version, inside the packs folder in the game, where default.pack is located.

I hope you enjoy the game and, if you do, please consider making a donation. It will help greatly!
