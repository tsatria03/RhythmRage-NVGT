When the dev puts text in quotes, the quote is a **reference**, not literal new content to insert. It is one of:
- something Claude said, that they're pointing back at, or
- something they **do** want, or something they **don't** want.

**Why:** a quoted phrase was once read as "add this text" when the dev was actually quoting the phrase they wanted *removed*.

**How to apply:** read the surrounding sentence to decide whether the quote marks a thing to keep/add or a thing to drop/avoid. Don't assume a quoted string is verbatim text to paste in. If genuinely ambiguous, ask. Related: [[ignore-terminal-commands]].
